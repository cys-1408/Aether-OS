# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct v2.1 (summary)

The Aether community is open, respectful and harassment-free. Be kind, assume good faith, disagree gracefully, and keep discussions technical. Reported violations go to conduct@aether-os.org and are reviewed by two maintainers; outcomes range from private warning to permanent ban from project spaces. The full policy text follows the Contributor Covenant 2.1 (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
